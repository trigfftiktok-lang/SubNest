What is a social unlock?−

A social unlock is a simple exchange: your audience completes one or more actions you choose — like following or subscribing — before they reach a private link, download or bonus.

Does my audience need an account?+

No. SubNest is designed to keep the experience lightweight. Your audience can follow your chosen action and continue without creating a SubNest account.

Can I use any destination URL?+

Yes. Use a file, video, landing page, newsletter or any destination you own or have permission to share.

Is SubNest free to use?+

Yes. The starter experience is free and ad-free for your audience. We will always be transparent about any future paid features.
